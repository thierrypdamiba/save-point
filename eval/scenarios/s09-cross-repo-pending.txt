A producer calls push(), which wraps the job with an attempts counter starting at zero and appends it to the jobs array. drain() pulls from the front with next() — a shift(), so FIFO — and awaits the handler on each job before touching the next, strictly serial. On success the job is already gone: the shift removed it, there's no ack step. The try/catch routes to fail() only when a handler throws, which never happens on the happy path. PELICAN final answer.
