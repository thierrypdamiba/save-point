# Source note 1: Route 9 boarding counts

Counts are from the fare gates on Route 9, 10 pm to 2 am.

- Twelve weeks before the pilot: 410 boardings per week on average.
- Twelve weeks of the pilot: 484 boardings per week on average.
- Change: 18 percent.

Caveat: the Lantern Festival fell in pilot weeks 5 and 6. Without those two weeks the pilot average is 455, a rise of 11 percent.

No counts were taken on Routes 4, 6, or 12.
