# Night Bus Pilot: Recommendation Brief

Prepared for the Tessmere Transit Desk.
All figures come from the three source notes in `sources/`.

## Summary

The Route 9 night bus pilot should be extended to all four night routes.

## Findings

1. Late-night boardings on Route 9 rose 18 percent over the twelve-week pilot.
2. Riders feel safer. In the rider survey, 62 percent of respondents said they felt safer traveling at night.
3. Cost per ride fell from 9.40 to 7.10 crowns.
4. At this rate the pilot will pay for itself within two years.

## Recommendation

Extend the night service to Routes 4, 6, 9, and 12 from the next schedule change.
